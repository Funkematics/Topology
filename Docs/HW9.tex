\problem Sutherland 10.6

Suppose that $(A, \mathcal{T}_A)$ is a subspace of a topological space $(X, \mathcal{T})$ and let $W \subseteq A$.
\begin{itemize}
  \item[(a)] If $W$ is open in $A$ (that is, if $W \in \mathcal{T}_A$) and $A$ is open in $X$ (that is, if $A \in \mathcal{T}$) then $W$ is open in $X$ (that is, $W \in \mathcal{T}$).
  \item[(b)] If $W$ is closed in $A$ and $A$ is closed in $X$ then $W$ is closed in $X$.
\end{itemize}

\problem Sutherland 10.7(b) Hint: Prop 9.5

Suppose that $f : X \to Y$ is a map of topological spaces. Prove that $f$ is continuous in each of the following cases.
\begin{itemize}
  \item[(b)] $X = \bigcup_{i=1}^{n} V_i$ where each $V_i$ is a closed subset of $X$ and $f|V_i$ is continuous for each $i$.
\end{itemize}
[Hint: Recall Exercise 3.13.]

\problem Sutherland 10.10

Suppose that $X$, $Y$ are spaces with subspaces $A \subseteq X$, $B \subseteq Y$, and that $f : X \to Y$ is a homeomorphism with $f(A) = B$. Prove that the maps $g : A \to B$ and $h : X \setminus A \to Y \setminus B$ induced by $f$ are both homeomorphisms.

\problem Sutherland 10.11

Suppose that $X$, $Y$ are topological spaces each with the discrete topology. Prove that the product topology for $X \times Y$ is discrete.

\problem Sutherland 10.15 (The first sentence should start ``Prove that \textbf{if}'')

\begin{itemize}
  \item[(a)] Prove that $W$ is open in a topological product $X \times Y$ then $p_X(W)$ is open in $X$ and $p_Y(W)$ is open in $Y$.
  \item[(b)] Give an example of a closed set $W \subseteq \Reals \times \Reals$ whose projection $p_1(W)$ on the $x$-axis is not closed in $\Reals$.
\end{itemize}

\problem Sutherland 10.16 (i) and (ii) \textbf{(AI Free)}

Suppose that $X$, $Y$ are spaces and that $A \subseteq X$, $B \subseteq Y$. Prove that
\begin{itemize}
  \item[(i)] the interior of $A \times B$ is $\mathring{A} \times \mathring{B}$,
  \item[(ii)] $\overline{A \times B} = \overline{A} \times \overline{B}$.
\end{itemize}

\problem Sutherland 10.17

Suppose that $X \times X$ is the topological product of a space $X$ with itself. Prove that the map $t : X \times X \to X \times X$ defined by $t(x, x') = (x', x)$ is a homeomorphism.
